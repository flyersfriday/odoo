Website Price Hide for Non Logged-In Users

This module hides product prices, quantity, and “Add to Cart” options from non logged-in users on your Odoo website.

Logged-in users can see all product details and purchase normally — perfect for B2B or restricted-access stores.

Figure: Website shop view for non logged-in user (price hidden)

Key Features

  • ✅ Hide product price for non logged-in (guest) users.
  • ✅ Hide quantity selector and “Add To Cart” button for guests.
  • ✅ Display full product info for logged-in users.
  • ✅ Seamless integration with Odoo Website & eCommerce modules.
  • ✅ Lightweight and configuration-free setup.
