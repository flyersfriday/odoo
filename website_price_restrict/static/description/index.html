Website Price Hide for Non Logged-In Users

This module allows you to restrict product price visibility on the website for users who are not logged in.

It hides product prices, quantity, and the "Add To Cart" button for guest users. Logged-in users can view full product details and purchase normally.

Perfect for B2B or exclusive eCommerce stores where price visibility should be limited to registered customers only.

Key Features

  • ✅ Hide product price for non logged-in (guest) users.
  • ✅ Hide quantity selector and "Add To Cart" button for guests.
  • ✅ Show full product details for logged-in users.
  • ✅ Seamlessly integrates with Odoo Website and eCommerce modules.
  • ✅ Simple, lightweight, and configuration-free.
